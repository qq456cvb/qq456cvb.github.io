excerpt: 'OCH3R is a unified framework for object-centric holistic 3D reconstruction from a single RGB image. In one forward pass, a transformer simultaneously predicts all object instances together with their 6D poses and detailed 3D reconstructions, producing per-pixel semantic embeddings, depth, object coordinates, and 3D Gaussian representations. By transforming Gaussians into canonical space with the predicted poses for supervision, it avoids expensive per-image label generation, and achieves state-of-the-art results on indoor benchmarks with fully feed-forward inference that scales independently of the number of objects.'
